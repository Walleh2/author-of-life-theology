Our History

Author of Life Institute of Theology was founded with a divine mandate to equip leaders for the body of Christ and the world at large. Over the years, we have remained committed to academic excellence and spiritual formation.

Our Mission

To provide Theological education rooted in God's word, equipping God's people for the work of Ministry in the World.

Our Vision

To raise faithful stewards for Christ to transform the world.

Our Core Values

  • Integrity
  • Excellence
  • Servant Leadership
  • Faith
  • Love

Our Belief

We believe the Bible is the inspired Word of God and the final authority in all matters of faith and life.
