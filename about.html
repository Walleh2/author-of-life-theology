Institutional Overview

Author of Life Institute of Theology is a Christ-centered theological institution committed to the training, spiritual formation, and empowerment of faithful stewards for effective ministry, leadership, missions, and societal transformation. The institution exists to provide sound biblical education that combines academic excellence, spiritual discipline, practical ministry experience, and ethical leadership development. Established to respond to the growing need for competent and spiritually mature Christian leaders, the institution equips students with theological knowledge, ministerial skills, and godly character necessary for impactful service in the church and society. The institution offers certificate, diploma, executive diploma, undergraduate, postgraduate and specialized ministerial training programmes designed to meet contemporary theological and leadership needs while remaining rooted in biblical truth.

Our Mission

To provide sound biblical and theological education through spiritual formation, academic excellence, leadership development, and practical ministry training for effective Christian service and societal transformation.

Our Vision

To become a leading theological institution raising faithful stewards and transformational leaders for the advancement of God’s Kingdom globally.

Our Core Values

  • 1. Biblical Fidelity Commitment to the authority, integrity, and sound teaching of the Word of God. — 2 Timothy 3:16-17
  • 2. Spiritual Formation Cultivating Christlike character, holiness, prayer, and intimacy with God. — Galatians 4:19
  • 3. Faithful Stewardship Training believers to responsibly manage God-given gifts, resources, opportunities, and calling. — 1 Corinthians 4:2
  • 4. Servant Leadership Raising leaders who lead with humility, compassion, integrity, and sacrifice. — Mark 10:43-45
  • 5. Academic Excellence Pursuing excellence in theological education, research, and ministerial competence. — Ecclesiastes 9:10
  • 6. Kingdom Impact Equipping students for transformational ministry and societal influence. — Matthew 5:13-16
  • 7. Integrity and Accountability Promoting honesty, transparency, discipline, and godly conduct. — Proverbs 11:3
  • 8. Evangelism and Missions Passion for soul winning, discipleship, and global missions. — Matthew 28:19-20
  • 9. Compassion and Service Demonstrating God’s love through practical service to humanity. — Galatians 5:13
  • 10. Unity and Community Building a learning environment marked by love, respect, fellowship, and collaboration. — Psalm 133:1

Core Mandate

    The institution seeks to:

  • 1. Provide quality theological and ministerial education rooted in Scripture.
  • 2. Train faithful stewards and transformational Christian leaders.
  • 3. Promote spiritual maturity, prayer, holiness, and Christian character.
  • 4. Equip students for pastoral ministry, missions, evangelism, and church leadership.
  • 5. Foster academic excellence and critical theological reflection.
  • 6. Encourage community development and social responsibility.
  • 7. Develop effective communication, leadership, and counseling skills.
  • 8. Prepare graduates for impactful service in church and society.
